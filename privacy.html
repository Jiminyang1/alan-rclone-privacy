Privacy Policy

alan-rclone is a personal backup tool used only by its owner. It accesses the owner's own Google Drive through rclone, running on the owner's own computers, to upload and download the owner's own files.

  • No data is collected, stored, sold or shared with anyone.
  • Google user data is only read or written on the owner's own machines, at the owner's request.
  • The app is not used by, or offered to, any other user.

Use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
